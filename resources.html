Timelines and costs: the wait

Becker's Hospital Review — Before You Cut Care: Fix the Cost Drain in Medical Credentialing and Enrollment

The clearest single statement of the cost: credentialing and payer enrollment delays cost health systems an estimated $10,122 per provider, per month, in deferred revenue, with timelines of 60 to 90 days for credentialing and longer for enrollment.

DoctorsManagement — Credentialing and Provider Enrollment: The Hidden Timeline

A stage-by-stage walk through the full path from CAQH setup to an effective participation date — commonly 90 to 180 days per payer — including the Medicare PECOS figures and the two-to-six-week contract loading step most estimates leave out.

Helpware — Provider Credentialing Services: Process, Costs & Vendors

A vendor-neutral guide to the seven stages of credentialing, the 90-to-120-day planning number for a clean commercial file, the $200-to-$500 per-payer outsourced price band, and the NCQA 180-day verification rule in force since July 1, 2025.

MD Revenue Group — How Long Does Provider Credentialing Take? Payer-by-Payer Breakdown

The payer-by-payer numbers — Medicare 60 to 90 days, commercial payers 90 to 150, some networks to 180 — alongside the MGMA findings that 67 percent of medical groups report 60-to-90-day onboarding delays and 72 percent report revenue loss tied to enrollment delays.

CarePatron — How Much Does Insurance Credentialing Cost? (2026, Real Numbers)

A plain accounting of what credentialing costs and what it does not: the CAQH portal is free for providers, outsourced work runs $100 to $300 per payer per provider, and full initial packages commonly run $2,500 to $5,000.

NexCure — Provider Credentialing Services: Complete Guide for Practices

A practice-side guide that separates credentialing, privileging, and enrollment into three timelines — including hospital privileging at 60 to 120 days — and warns that sequential submission is how a four-month process becomes an eight-month one.

Machinery: the portal, the standards, the compact

Sullivan McG — Credentialing Software Cost vs Hiring Credentialing Staff

The best single source for the CAQH figures: a survey of 1,240 physician practices showing an average of 20.2 health plan contracts, and a monthly cost of $2,068 for practices using multiple methods to exchange credentialing information against $1,249.86 for those using one platform.

ReliableCare — How to Set Up a CAQH Profile: Step-by-Step Guide

A working guide to the CAQH Provider Data Portal — the application accepted in all 50 states, used by more than 1,000 health plans, re-attested every 120 days — including the June 2026 rebrand to DataSpring.

Credentially — Interstate Medical Licensure Compact: A Credentialing Guide

A close reading of the Compact's own figures: 44 member jurisdictions as of October 31, 2026, an average of 4.0 licenses per applicant in the Year 9 data study, and the states that remain outside it.

Interstate Medical Licensure Compact Commission — FY2025 Annual Report

The Commission's own report for July 2024 to June 2025: more than 49,000 physicians using the process, more than 170,000 licenses issued, and 44 member jurisdictions.

Vendors: who is building the software

Forbes — This Health Startup Aims To Cut Down A Burdensome Chunk Of Doctors' Paperwork

Amy Feldman's August 2025 profile of Medallion and its founder Derek Lo: about $130 million raised at an estimated $440 million valuation, contracted annual revenue approaching $50 million, and the Tampa General result — credentialing turnaround from 30 days to a median of 3 days.

Verifiable — Verifiable Raises $27M Series B

The company's own announcement of its July 2023 Series B led by Craft Ventures, with customers including Humana, Talkspace, Modern Health, and Headway, and the claim that its verification layer shortens credentialing from weeks to days.

Healthcare IT Today — Certify Secures $40M Series B Funding

The July 2025 announcement of CertifyOS's $40 million Series B led by Transformation Capital, bringing its total to $69 million, with the company's scale claims — 1,600-plus data points per provider and more than 15 million licenses managed.

Physicians Practice — UnitedHealthcare, Cigna and Centene Sign On to Shared Credentialing Program

The September 2025 announcement of CertifyOS's National Shared Credentialing Program, with the three largest participating plans named, the company's $2 billion annual verification-cost claim, and the MGMA Stat poll finding that 32 percent of medical groups reported a credentialing backlog.

Cooley — Clearlake to Acquire Symplr

The 2018 announcement of Clearlake Capital's acquisition of symplr from Pamlico Capital and CapStreet, describing a platform serving more than 1,500 customers and 4,000 healthcare facilities with provider management, credential management, and payer enrollment.

PitchBook — Medallion Company Profile

PitchBook's funding record for Medallion, including the $40 million debt refinancing in March 2026, the Series C in August 2025, and the September 2026 acquisition of Andros.
